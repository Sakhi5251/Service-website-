How EquipLink Africa Works

A simple connection between equipment owners and people who need machinery.

1

Post

Equipment owners advertise their machinery, while borrowers post their equipment requirements.

2

Discover

Users search for equipment or equipment requirements based on their needs and location.

3

Connect

Interested parties communicate directly and discuss availability, pricing and project details.
