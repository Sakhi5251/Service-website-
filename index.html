How EquipLink Africa Works

One platform connecting people who need machinery with people who have machinery.

🔎

1. Find Equipment

Customers advertise the equipment they need and owners can respond with available machinery.

🚜

2. Advertise Machinery

Equipment owners can advertise excavators, trucks, loaders, graders, tractors and other machinery.

🤝

3. Connect

EquipLink Africa helps both sides connect directly and discuss pricing, availability, location and requirements.

🏗️

4. Get the Job Done

Equipment can be arranged for construction, agriculture, mining, earthworks, transport and other projects.
